About Social Work Program

Global Definition of the Social Work Profession

Social work is a practice-based profession and an academic discipline that promotes social change and development, social cohesion, and the empowerment and liberation of people. Principles of social justice, human rights, collective responsibility and respect for diversities are central to social work. Underpinned by theories of social work, social sciences, humanities and indigenous knowledges, social work engages people and structures to address life challenges and enhance wellbeing. The above definition may be amplified at national and/or regional levels.

UNO-R Bachelor of Science in Social Work (BSSW)

The Bachelor of Science in Social Work readies professionals who are globally competitive and dedicated to promoting social justice, human dignity, and empowerment within communities. Rooted in Catholic Christian principles, the program provides students with the essential knowledge, skills, and ethical grounding needed to tackle intricate social challenges and encourage sustainable growth. Graduates are equipped to spearhead transformative projects that enhance the well-being and comprehensive development of individuals, families, and communities.

WHERE CAN A BSSW DEGREE TAKE YOU?

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES FOR BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN SOCIAL WORK (BSSW) GRADUATES IN THE PHILIPPINES[cite: 12]

The Bachelor of Science in Social Work (BSSW) prepares graduates for generalist social work practice, enabling them to work with individuals, families, groups, organizations, and communities across various settings[cite: 12]. In the Philippines, social work is a regulated profession[cite: 12]. Graduates may pursue careers in government, non-government organizations, hospitals, schools, residential facilities, community development, and other human-service institutions[cite: 12]. A BSSW graduate who passes the SWLE and obtains the required professional registration may pursue social work positions across diverse settings[cite: 12].
