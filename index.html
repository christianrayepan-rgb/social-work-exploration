About Social Work Program

About Social Work

Social work is a regulated profession in the Philippines committed to nurturing and improving essential social relationships while enhancing social functioning[cite: 1]. Its objective is to promote holistic individual and community development through proven methodologies[cite: 1].

BS Social Work Program

The Bachelor of Science in Social Work (BSSW) prepares graduates for generalist practice with individuals, families, groups, and communities[cite: 1]. Rooted in Catholic Christian values, it equips students to pass the Social Work Licensure Examination (SWLE)[cite: 1].

Career Opportunities in the Philippines & Beyond

Where Can a BSSW Degree Take You?

Government Social Welfare

DSWD, LGUs, Municipal & City Offices

Key Roles: Social Welfare Officer, Municipal Social Welfare Officer, Case Manager, Disaster Emergency Worker, Program Coordinator.

NGOs & Civil Society

Non-Profits, Foundations & Advocacy Groups

Key Roles: Project Officer, Community Development Worker, Child Protection Officer, MEAL Specialist.

Medical & Healthcare

Public/Private Hospitals & Rehab Centers

Key Roles: Medical Social Worker, Patient Support Worker, Hospital Case Manager, Patient Assistance Officer.

Child Welfare & Residential Care

Residential Facilities & Adoption Agencies

Key Roles: Child & Family Social Worker, Residential Worker, Adoption/Foster Care Worker, Reintegration Support.

Schools & Youth Services

Educational Institutions & Student Affairs

Key Roles: Student Services Officer, Youth Development Officer, School Social Worker, Student Support Case Worker.

DRRM & Humanitarian Response

Disaster Agencies & Humanitarian NGOs

Key Roles: Disaster Response Worker, Humanitarian Case Worker, Emergency Assistance Officer, Psychosocial Support Worker.

Community Dev. & Advocacy

Barangays, IP Communities & Rural Programs

Key Roles: Community Organizer, Social Mobilization Officer, Human Rights Officer, Livelihood Project Officer.

Specialized Populations

Senior Centers, PWD Programs, Rehab Services

Key Roles: Disability Inclusion Officer, Older Persons Welfare Worker, Gender-Based Violence Worker, Reintegration Specialist.

Research & Policy Analysis

Research Institutes & Policy Groups

Key Roles: Social Research Assistant, Policy Research Officer, Program Evaluator, Social Development Researcher.

International Dev. & Academia

Global INGOs, Academic Institutions & Consultancy

Key Roles: Protection Project Officer, Fieldwork Coordinator, Social Work Educator, International Humanitarian Staff.
