About Social Work Program

Global Definition of Social Work

Social work is a practice-based profession and an academic discipline that promotes social change and development, social cohesion, and the empowerment and liberation of people.

UNO-R BSSW Program

The Bachelor of Science in Social Work readies professionals who are globally competitive and dedicated to promoting social justice, human dignity, and empowerment within communities.
